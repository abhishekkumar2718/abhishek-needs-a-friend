# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Static GitHub Pages hosting in a public repository, set up with the user's GitHub CLI.

## Users

Abhishek presents the deck during a humorous speech; the audience watches the slides.

## Product Purpose

A ten-slide joke presentation of friendship survey responses.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Use a dark theme and minimal unobstructive styling. Titles: "Friendship Survey", "Acquisition Funnel", "Spectrum of Emotions", "Feedback Sentiment", "Key Feedback", and "Net Promoter Score", all with standard slide-title styling. Acquisition Funnel follows the survey screenshot and presents response outcomes as percentages, without disclosing the five-person sample: 60% replied, 20% said “Absolutely!” and then forgot, and 20% did not open the messages. It has no closing line. A title-only "Spectrum of Emotions" slide is followed by separate, immediately visible Embarrassment and Glee response slides. Emotion captions preserve the user's supplied copy: "Embarrassment" and "Glee". Both glee messages appear together, with the interest/roast screenshot first. Feedback Sentiment contains the illustrative 85% negative / 15% positive pie and a numbers-free category legend. Key Feedback follows it with the single recurring improvement theme "Reading the room" on the left and "Available" under Strengths on the right. Net Promoter Score contains "1 in 5 respondents recommend Abhishek as a friend." as a hero stat. A final "Thanks" slide is centered horizontally and vertically. User supplied all four screenshots and the recommendation stat. Keep whole-slide keyboard navigation without reveal steps. Iterate locally first; publish only on explicit user request.

## Brand Commitments

No additional jokes, decorative elements, or marketing copy. Keep the screenshots uncropped.
